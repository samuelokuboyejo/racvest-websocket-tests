Racvest · Delivery flowCourier on the left, sender on the right. Follow one delivery from request to drop-off.
How to run it: connect both sides; the courier starts sending heartbeats so they are available. Create a delivery request as the sender (Postman or the app). The courier gets it and the delivery ID fills in by itself. Bid as the courier, accept as the sender, then the courier switches to live tracking: pick a destination on the courier map and press Drive, and the sender's map follows. Tokens and IDs are the ones the other pages save.
SYSReady. Connect the courier on the left and the sender on the right.
